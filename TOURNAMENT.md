# Tournament — How Many Tabs Do You Have Open?

Judged head-to-head with the competition's own judge brief, in its
priority order: (1) reads as a real count of open browser tabs; (2)
exactly one number in digits; (3) surprising — far higher, far lower, or
stranger than the field would guess; (4) believable as an honest answer,
not an absurd figure picked to shock; (5) hints at a life behind it.
"Size for its own sake does not count." Margin 1 (by a hair) to 5
(overwhelmingly better), as the house judge scores. Each pair was judged
on the merits from both sides; where the first differing criterion did
not decide it, a later criterion differing "by much more" did, per the
brief.
